How this works

Every day at 00:00 UTC a new round appears: a handful of feeder puzzles and one metapuzzle. Each feeder hides a single word, and nothing tells you how. The flavour text, the title, and the shape of the data are the only clues. Some puzzles have more than one layer.

The meta uses the feeder answers (and sometimes things you noticed along the way) to produce one final, on-theme word. Answers are checked against a hash, so the page itself doesn't contain them. Solutions for each round are published the day after.

Hints exist, but using one is noted on your record.
